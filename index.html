About Winnie's

Expert braiding with a commitment to quality, comfort, and style.

Craftsmanship You Can Trust

At Winnie's Hair Braiding, we specialize in protective styles that look stunning and feel comfortable. With years of experience and locations in both New York and Minnesota, our team serves local clients and welcomes international visitors looking for premium braiding services.

We use quality hair extensions, keep a clean and relaxing salon environment, and work around your schedule — because beauty shouldn't wait.

Our Policies

Know before you book — our customer and braider policies keep every appointment safe, fair, and on time.

Read the full policy text

Customer Policy

  1. Appointments are required. Walk-ins are accepted when available.
  2. A non-refundable deposit may be required to secure your appointment.
  3. Please arrive on time. Late arrivals of 15 minutes or more may need to reschedule.
  4. Hair must be clean, dry, and free of heavy oils and product buildup before your appointment.
  5. Bring the correct hair if it is not included with your service.
  6. Full payment is due immediately after your service is completed.
  7. We accept approved payment methods only.
  8. No extra guests unless previously approved.
  9. Children must be supervised at all times.
  10. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel or reschedule.
  11. Prices vary based on style, hair length, size, and complexity.
  12. We are not responsible for allergic reactions to products. Please inform us of any allergies before your service.
  13. Please inspect your hairstyle before leaving the salon. Any concerns should be discussed before departure.
  14. We reserve the right to refuse service for disrespectful, abusive, or inappropriate behavior.
  15. Thank you for respecting our time, policies, and business.

Braider Policy

  1. Arrive on time and be ready before your first appointment.
  2. Wear clean, professional attire and maintain good personal hygiene.
  3. Treat every client with respect, courtesy, and professionalism.
  4. Keep your workstation clean before, during, and after each service.
  5. Sanitize all tools and equipment after every client.
  6. Follow all salon pricing — do not negotiate prices without management approval.
  7. Do not accept personal payments or side jobs from salon clients.
  8. Protect client privacy and keep all information confidential.
  9. Use salon products and supplies responsibly.
  10. Report damaged equipment or low inventory immediately.
  11. No smoking, alcohol, or drug use while working.
  12. Do not use offensive language or argue with clients or coworkers.
  13. Ask for assistance if a client has concerns or complaints.
  14. Follow all salon safety, sanitation, and customer service standards.
  15. Represent Winnie African Hair Braiding with honesty, professionalism, and excellence every day.
